## Rules

- Do this task only. If you find other work, note it under Gotchas; don't do it.
- Write code, docs and your own evidence file. Everything else in the workstream folder, and the registry, belongs to the coordinator: never edit them.
- A reversible judgment call goes in your evidence as `### P1: the call`, with the reason and the rejected alternative. Never invent a `D` number.
- If `ws verify` can't go green, or the task needs a decision that isn't yours, stop guessing: write `- **Status:** blocked (the reason)`, commit that, and report.
- Don't merge. Don't switch to the integration branch.
