## Proposed decisions

### P1: `Scenario` also has `why_not: Callable[[str], str]`, which gives the reason on the N/A line
D-APPLIES gives `applies` as a bool, but the N/A line needs a reason, and only the scenario knows what its premise is. So `text`'s `why_not` reads the adapter: "forbids no folder" when `cannot_edit` is empty, and "forbids X, not .claude/" when it names other folders. `applies` checks that `TEXT_FILE`'s top folder (`.claude/`) is listed in `raw["worker"]["cannot_edit"]`, and is always true for the stub. The preflight still runs before the loop, even when every scenario chosen is N/A. Rejected: having `applies` return a reason string, which changes D-APPLIES's signature; and writing text-specific wording into `main`.
