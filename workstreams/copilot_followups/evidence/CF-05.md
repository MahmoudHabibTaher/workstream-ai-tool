## Proposed decisions

### D009: `hook.py` is unchanged, because the real Copilot payload matches the shape it already reads

The captured payload has `toolArgs` as an object holding `path`, which `hook.paths_in` already reads. The extra fields (`sessionId`, `timestamp`, `toolCallId`, `old_str`, `new_str`) don't matter to it. Rejected: widening the parser or the matcher on the strength of one run. The scope rule also forbids changing the matcher without a recorded payload that needs it.
