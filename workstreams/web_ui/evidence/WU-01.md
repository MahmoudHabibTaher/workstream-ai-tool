## Proposed decisions

### D002: the queue is every reviewable task in an open workstream, with no notion of "reviewed"
Nothing in the tracking files says a person has reviewed a task, and adding it would be a new kind of write. The page keeps its own per-browser marks, keyed by `revision`. Rejected: a `reviewed` field on the task written through `ws act`, which is a tracked fact nothing else would read.

## Gotchas and follow-ups

`--checks` writes loose objects through `verify.code_tree`'s throwaway index, as `ws settle --dry-run` does. It doesn't touch the real index.
