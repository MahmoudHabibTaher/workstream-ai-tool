## Proposed decisions

### P1: an entry that doesn't fit is skipped, and later shorter ones are still kept

D-GOTCHA says to keep whole entries up to the cap but not whether to stop at the first that doesn't fit. Going on keeps more at the same cost, and T5's complaint was short late gotchas being lost. So an entry is cut only when no entry fits whole; alongside one that fits, an over-limit entry is left out and counted. Rejected: stopping at the first entry that doesn't fit, since one long bullet would hide every short one after it.

### P2: a cut entry counts as one in `(N more in <path>)`

D-GOTCHA asks for the same pointer on a cut entry, and `(0 more ...)` would say nothing was left out. So N counts entries not shown whole, and a lone cut entry reads `(1 more in <path>)`. Rejected: a different wording for the cut case, which D-GOTCHA rules out.
