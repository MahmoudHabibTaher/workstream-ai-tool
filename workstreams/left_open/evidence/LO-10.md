The refusals and the commit-and-restore path are the same for both, so `_correct_acceptance` now passes its label and write into `_correct`, and its existing tests still pass unchanged. The brief asks only for the --because refusal to name it, but `--commit needs a hash, except with --acceptance` would be false once --summary takes a bare --commit. Rejected: leaving that wording as it was.
