## Gotchas and follow-ups

- `tasks.save` also sorts tasks by priority and restamps `schemaVersion`. A queue on disk that isn't already in that form counts as changed, so a same-value `--commit` on it would commit the normalisation rather than say nothing changed. Not reachable through the CLI's own writes, and left as it is.
